\section{Methodology}
For each month $t$, operating profit, profit margin and revenue growth are
\begin{align}
P_t &= R_t - E_t, \\
M_t &= \frac{P_t}{R_t} \times 100\%, \\
G &= \frac{R_{\text{last}} - R_{\text{first}}}{R_{\text{first}}} \times 100\%.
\end{align}
